**Full description**

SlideJam is a calm traffic logic puzzle. Each vehicle can only slide along its own lane. Shuffle cars, buses and trucks out of the way and drive the red target car through the EXIT.

★ 20 hand-checked traffic puzzles, each verified solvable
★ Undo and Redo, plus a Hint that shows a good move
★ Clear a jam in the fewest possible moves for 3 stars
★ Daily reward calendar (7 days) and a new Daily challenge every day
★ Skin Shop: road themes and car paint. Tap to preview, or try any skin free for 24 hours
★ Achievements and a Stats page
★ Tutorial on level 1 and a How to play card
★ Full keyboard and screen-reader support, plus reduce motion, larger text and high contrast
★ Fully offline

Fair play: coins are cosmetic only. They're earned by playing and can never be bought or cashed out. Ads are optional rewarded videos you choose to watch (extra hints, 24h skin trials), plus an occasional ad between levels after your first session. Never on launch.
